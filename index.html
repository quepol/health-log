Health Log

Track headaches, exercise or anything else about your health with one tap, and spot patterns over time. Free, no account, and your data stays on your phone.

Install it in 3 steps

  1. Tap Share (bottom of the screen in Safari, top right in Chrome)
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Health Log from your Home Screen

Opening from the Home Screen saves your data on your phone instead of your browser.

What do you want to keep track of?

Symptoms, exercise, anything you want to keep an eye on. Each one becomes a button you tap when it happens. You can change these any time in Settings → What you track.

Details you can add

After you tap a button, you can add details like these. We’ve started you with two. Tap to add or remove, or type your own below. You can change them any time in Settings → Details.

What you track

Track anything you're curious about, not just symptoms: exercise, medicine, a late meal. Tap it each time it happens. To see whether something tends to come before something else, make it a button rather than a detail.

Details

Details are optional extras you can add after tapping a button, like Stress or Poor sleep. Patterns shows which ones come up most for each thing you track. Hide any you don't use; past entries keep them.

Data

Everything stays on this device. Export regularly: it is your backup. Import merges and skips entries you already have.
