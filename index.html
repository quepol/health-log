Health Log

Add it to your Home Screen first

In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Health Log from the new icon. Set it up there: the Home Screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari.

What do you want to track?

Pick what applies to you. Each one becomes a button you tap when it happens. You can change these any time in Settings.

Possible triggers

After you log something, these appear so you can tap any that apply. You can edit them later in Settings.

Items

Tip: log exposures (Alcohol, Bad sleep, Skipped meal…) as items too. Patterns can then check which of them tend to come before a symptom.
