Items

Count days suits things that come and go within a day, like a headache. Episodes suits flare-ups that last days, like a cold sore: tap it once each day it’s there, and days in a row are grouped into one episode.

Tip: log exposures (Alcohol, Bad sleep, Skipped meal…) as items too. Patterns can then check which of them tend to come before a symptom.
